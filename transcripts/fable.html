Mythos-class

Claude Fable 5 is the public-facing version of Anthropic's Mythos model line, launched June 9, 2026 — a different line from Opus/Sonnet/Haiku. Mythos 5 itself is restricted to approved organizations. Fable's control surface is a reasoning-effort selector only (default High); unlike the Opus line, there is no Adaptive or Extended-thinking toggle. All runs here were taken at the default effort.

Limitation

Fallback architecture. In high-risk topic areas Fable blocks and silently falls back to Claude Opus 4.8; Anthropic reports ≥95% of sessions run entirely on Fable. No interface indicator shows which model answered a given response. The Carwash prompt does not plausibly trigger the fallback, but the caveat applies to every Fable entry as a class: any individual response could in principle have been served by Opus 4.8.
